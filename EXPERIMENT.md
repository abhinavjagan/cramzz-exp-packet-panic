# Experiment brief: Packet Panic

## Hypothesis

A deterministic, 30–60 second daily routing puzzle will earn repeat play and sharing among developers and infrastructure-curious people, creating credible, non-intrusive sponsor inventory.

## Audience and core loop

- Primary: network engineers, SREs, platform engineers, developers, and computer-science learners.
- Enter without an account, inspect a small directed network, and choose one hop at a time.
- Deliver within six hops, compare latency/reliability/score, then share an emoji-only route summary and challenge link.
- Return on the next UTC day to extend a device-local streak.

## Monetization test

| Package | Price | Inventory | Unlock rule |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Founding Node | ₹499 / 30 days | 20 total bookings; max 7 concurrent | Available at launch; later buyers receive the next non-overlapping window |
| Daily Challenge Sponsor | ₹1,999 / day | 1 per day | 250 completed plays |
| Season Sponsor | ₹9,999 / 30 days | 1 | 1,000 completed plays |

All placements are labelled. Seven eligible routers can carry a sponsor at once. The twenty-unit inventory is a lifetime booking cap, not twenty simultaneous placements: once all seven routers are occupied, later buyers receive the next non-overlapping 30-day window. A sponsor may name or decorate a router but cannot alter topology, link health, scoring, difficulty, player results, or shared-result language.

Sponsor flow: application → manual creative and destination review → individual Razorpay Payment Link → dashboard confirmation that payment is captured → placement. A callback or return URL is not proof of payment.

## Fixed fourteen-day gate

- **Scale:** 300 unique visitors and at least one of 8% sharing, 10% returning players, or one paid sponsor.
- **Iterate once:** 100 unique visitors plus one strong engagement signal, or three qualified sponsor replies.
- **Kill:** fewer than 100 visitors after seven original X posts, 35 relevant replies, and 20 individually researched sponsor approaches actually sent after approval, with no payment and no qualified sponsor interest.
- **Immediate kill:** meaningful legal, moderation, deceptive-design, security, or uncontrolled-cost risk.

The first revenue milestone is five Founding Nodes: ₹2,495 gross. Thresholds must not be changed after results are known.

## Constraints

- Maximum three build days and fourteen validation days.
- No paid infrastructure, advertising, SaaS upgrade, or physical materials.
- No accounts, server-side player profiles, automated DMs, engagement automation, or unreviewed sponsor creative.
- No auto-upgrades. Pause collection or features before a free service limit creates spend.
